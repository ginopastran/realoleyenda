Historias que parecen inventadas.

Real o Leyenda es un canal de shorts en castellano con leyendas, mitos y hechos reales que cuestan creer, contados en un minuto y en pixel art animado.

Real o Leyenda subidas

English

Real o Leyenda is a Spanish-language YouTube Shorts channel about legends, myths and true stories that are hard to believe, told in animated pixel art. “Real o Leyenda subidas” is the channel's internal tool to upload and schedule its own videos through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner on their own account and is not offered to the public.

It uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
